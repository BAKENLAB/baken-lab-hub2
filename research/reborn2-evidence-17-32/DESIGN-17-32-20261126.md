No silent normalization of class, track or distance; JRA/LOCAL separate.

Use latest 5 starts for current view, 10 for trend and all eligible condition comparisons with actual count; no cherry-picking or accumulation by history length. Each item outputs evidence IDs, measured/derived/proxy/missing status, observed fact, hypothesis, alternatives, present applicability, uncertainty, exclusions, dependency groups. Separate all-field context from individual observation. No invented seconds, distances, speed, effort, causal attribution, counterfactual finish, probability or grade. Distinguish official reported facts from observed proxies. No report does not imply clean trip. DNF, disqualification, dead heat, missing values and incomplete corner sequences remain explicit.

Corner intervals: for field N>=2 and position p in [lo,hi], normalized forward placement interval [(N-hi)/(N-1),(N-lo)/(N-1)]. Use actual comparable starters and documented field changes; do not silently count scratched runners or assume DNF remained active. Change from A to B expressed in ranks [A.lo-B.hi,A.hi-B.lo]; positive lower bound supports definite positional improvement, negative upper bound definite loss, otherwise direction uncertain. Compare only named chronological positions with consistent participants, not arbitrary array indexes. A rank change is not traveled distance, speed or passing every horse in between. Finish position is separate from corner rank. Race laps reflect leader/race progression, not individual split; first segment may be shorter than 200 m, never compare unequal lengths as if equal. Validate exact segment lengths and official total against winning clock, explicitly handle rounding and inconsistent sources. Pace context requires condition-matched, pre-cutoff benchmark with sample sizes and uncertainty; no universal fast/slow cutoff. Relative final3f uses same-race comparable observed finishers, completeness and measurement kind; lower time is faster, not proof of unused energy.

## Item designs
### 17 各コーナーの通過順位
Purpose: reconstruct observed positioning without inventing continuous trajectory. Input: official named corner groups or verified exact horse positions, complete field and identity. Output chronological named corner records with bounds, normalized placement, missing positions and field definition. NAR group bounds are preserved; JRA numeric array requires matching official named corner layout. No first-corner-as-start-speed inference. Tests: two runners tied in a group, missing C2, different layouts, duplicate horse number, scratched/DNF field.

### 18 道中の位置取り変化
Purpose: identify position gain/loss, stable path and uncertain moves. Reuse 17; calculate interval deltas for adjacent known comparable corners and last corner to classified finish separately (do not bridge missing corner and claim adjacent movement). Normalize across field sizes only with verified denominators. Show chronology, not additive gain across nonadjacent segments. Tests: uncertain overlap, definite improvement, no corners, DNF, field-size changes.

### 19 実ラップ・ペース
Purpose: race pace context for all runners. Use official leader lap sequence, segment distances, winning clock, course and condition benchmark from 05/43–57; calculate equal-distance section summaries and comparable benchmark deviations only if valid. Preserve actual pace profile early/mid/late and source kind. Without benchmark return measured lap sequence with pace classification unknown; individual effort not measured. Tests: partial first segment, missing lap, inconsistent total, slow/fast benchmark uncertainty, leader change, same-race packet reuse.

### 20 過去走の展開有利・不利
Purpose: evaluate how a documented race shape aligned with observed running position, not assign a blanket winner/loser bonus. Combine 17–19, final3f, field finish distribution and comparable race pace; compare outcome of early-position cohorts descriptively, keep current horse's outcome out of cohort evidence when feasible, show sample sizes. Strong front pace + forward runner maintaining performance vs other forward runners can support adversity hypothesis; slow pace + isolated rear runner recovering can support alternative adversity. Consider ability/class/traffic/condition; no automatic pace-position rule. If benchmark or cohort incomplete return unknown/mixed. Tests: dominant winner despite quick pace, all front runners collapse, single closer wins, small field, incomplete pace.

### 21 道中での後退からの巻き返し
Purpose: detect observed decline followed by recovery. Require at least three distinct chronological observations or two corners plus finish (label finish-based recovery), interval-confirmed loss then gain, consistent field and no inferred intermediate trajectory. Record worst observed interval and final outcome/clock gap; corroborate with final3f where present. No incident inference or bonus for losing position deliberately. Tests: 3→8→3, overlapping groups, 3→8→DNF, unobserved intermediate corner, group resolution.

### 22 コーナーでの進出
Purpose: detect improvement between named corner observations. Use 17/18 interval deltas, note segment endpoints, distinguish gain into a corner from gain inside a corner: ordinary corner observations prove only between-observation movement. Course shape/position of observation must be verified before distance-specific interpretation. No inside/outside path or overtaking speed from position alone. Tests: 8→4, 8→[4,8], missing label, corner-to-finish only, differing fields.

### 23 先行争いによる消耗
Purpose: identify evidence consistent with effort from early pace competition. Need observed early forward positions and rival proximity only when official group/spacing data supports it, matched early race pace from 19, later position/clock/final3f and opposing forward cohort. Use hypothesis labels, alternatives include distance suitability, class, documented incident, tactics; first rank alone is not a duel, close rank not measured physical spacing. No quantification of individual energy. Tests: uncontested leader, fast early pace with durable leader, grouped leaders with no spacing, later fade with missing pace.

### 24 終盤の失速原因
Purpose: characterize late deterioration and separately identify supported explanations. Last named observations to finish with interval deltas, elapsed gap, same-race final3f and pace; distance fatigue 11, official incident 26, comparable class and condition. Output observed deterioration and candidate explanations separately; multiple/missing explanations possible. Only explicit official report supports reported event, not necessarily sole causal explanation. DNF is status, not extreme slow clock. Tests: slow relative final3f but maintained rank, rank loss due to strong closers, pace pressure, documented incident, DNF, missing final3f.

### 25 差し損ね・脚の余力
Purpose: identify late recovery that did not reach leaders. Require classified finish, last observed position, relative final3f and gap to winner, race shape. Show late gain, close or wide gap with condition/field context, not fixed universal threshold. Fastest final3f alone is not proof of unused energy or unlucky run. '脚の余力' remains hypothesis, never measured reserve unless separately legitimate evidence exists. Distinguish too-far-back, pace setup, verified traffic, ability deficit as alternatives. Tests: fastest final3f yet wide gap, narrow gap and late gain, no pace, forward winner, explicit traffic.

### 26 出遅れ・進路などの不利
Purpose: official incident/event registry. JRA 競走中の出来事等 and linked steward report, NAR official result's 裁決レポート/パトロール; existing incident witness intake is not a full parser. Require exact race, horse ID, source document and publish version/date/time, original finding, type (late start, interference suffered, obstruction, stumble, etc.), distinguish victim/causer, phase only when stated, official reported vs unverified candidate. Do not infer from rank or slow time, no unreported=>normal. Video not automated unless permitted and independently validated. Tests: same names, two horses mentioned with different roles, negated finding, post-cutoff document, missing report, amended finding.

### 27 不利を克服した好走
Purpose: link 26 verified suffered event with subsequent observed performance. Require chronological relation if event phase known, later position/finish/gap/final3f and class/pace benchmark or rival context; avoid claiming recovery from ambiguous timing. Strong finish after reported adversity supports resilience hypothesis; causation and seconds lost unknown. No double credit for 26 and 27. Tests: offender not victim, incident after last observed corner, DNF, narrow loss despite report, report with no temporal link.

### 28 僅差の凡走
Purpose: distinguish modest finish from competitiveness. Use finish and classified field, official literal margin and rounded winner gap 03 separately, next gap 04, class and pace context. Define '凡走' and '僅差' only with later calibrated context-aware comparison; report gap distribution/relative finish, no fixed cutoff or automatic excuse. Preserve equal rounded clocks with distinct literal margins. Tests: tightly bunched field, identical rounded times, large winner gap despite narrow neighbor gap, DNF, dead heat.

### 29 負けて強し
Purpose: build evidence chain that a defeat nevertheless demonstrates transferable ability. Require observed non-win and verified evidence: near winner against strong rivals (33–42), pace adversity (20), recovery (21/27), late gain (25) or comparable clock level (05/13); alternative explanations and current relevance mandatory. Single strong chain may be a supported hypothesis, not mandatory count/grade. Same race event used by 20/23/24/29 not independent votes. Later rival results excluded unless public before target cutoff. Tests: weak class narrow defeat, strong opposition with wider gap, favorable setup, later opponent win forbidden, duplicate evidence.

### 30 勝利内容の価値
Purpose: judge strength of demonstrated win, not count wins. Require official classified win (including explicit dead heat as such), winner clock vs comparable baseline, runner-up gap/literal margin, opposition level, race shape, position and final3f context, weight/conditions. Distinguish favorable setup from win achieved despite adversity; no automatic superiority from large margin or slow win. If later items unavailable, output raw win context and unknown opponent/transfer value. Tests: wide margin weak rivals, narrow strong win, advantageous uncontested lead, dead heat, missing baseline.

### 31 特殊条件による凡走
Purpose: identify condition-associated poor performance and test applicability today. Define poor performance relative to horse's verified comparable baseline (13), not finishing rank alone; candidate special conditions include atypical distance/surface/going/layout/class/pace, layoff or official incident where verified. Compare relevant history independently selected by conditions, sample size and competing explanations. One run supports hypothesis only; never discard automatically, never treat condition change as proof of rebound. Test return to conditions with current ability evidence (15/43–57/93–100). Tests: repeated poor under same condition, atypical race with strong clock, no comparable sample, today's conditions still adverse, multiple simultaneous changes.

### 32 前走からの内容改善
Purpose: assess change in performance quality from immediately preceding starts without outcome leakage. Compare actual chronological most recent two eligible starts with their conditions, adjusted residual, winner gap, relative final3f, observed position and context; record chronological most recent official start even if unusable, never substitute earlier favorable start as if last. Distinguish direct pairwise change (32) from multi-run trend (14). If unlike conditions cannot be reconciled, MIXED/INSUFFICIENT; direction per feature, no composite score. Tests: worse finish better clock in higher class, easier opposition win, missing last start, incomparable surface, tiny change within measurement resolution.
