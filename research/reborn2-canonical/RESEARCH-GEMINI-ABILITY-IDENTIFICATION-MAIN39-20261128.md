## Corrections and implementation criteria
- Ordinal position increase does not identify individual acceleration, rival deceleration, physical gain or path loss. Fast final3f is an average over interval, not acceleration. Require at least two comparable consecutive individual interval speed observations (distance/time, timestamps, measurement uncertainty and geometry/path scope) to support speed-change claim; synchronized peer gaps/speeds for relative progress decomposition. If only rank, report rank change; if gaps, relative gain; if only final3f, terminal average observation. Race-leader laps not individual interval times. Visual observations can support qualitative hypotheses if authenticated/scope known, not manufacture precise measurements. Both horses may accelerate or decelerate at different rates.
- Early position is not absolute acceleration. Separate reaction/start stability, initial speed, second acceleration and tactical intent; compare observed checkpoints and gate, distance, condition. Class rise/distance shortening alone does not prove faster pace or pursuit pressure. 
- Pressure tolerance requires actual pressure evidence and comparison with relevant conditions; contact, pace and proximity not identical. Sustained speed vs low-load persistence distinct, no causal proof by outcome alone. Reacceleration requires measured speed change or carefully bounded qualitative evidence; being passed then regaining rank does not prove. Finishing ability and winning outcome separate; a nonwinning horse may have strong terminal capability and a winner may have favorable setup. Distance vs pace confounded by class, going, course, weight, opponents, health and tactics; matched comparison and unknowns.
