## Offline CSV handoff implemented
nar-monthly-materials.mjs reuses the CSV parser and common archive 01–12 packet builder. It accepts caller-provided UTF-8 BOM bytes, independently pinned 66/36-column headers, an acquisition receipt, and exact prior-result plus profile witnesses. The upstream witness must establish stable IDs. The module checks those IDs against the current roster and binds result URL/race/number, name, birth, pedigree, finish, and cutoff. It does not fetch or independently authenticate a caller's witness. Its packet records file hashes and parsed row counts. Payback is outside the input contract.

Same-day and future horse rows are excluded. An incomplete result, unknown status, or unresolved winner clock leaves gaps and laps unset. Timed full fields supply rounded clock gaps separately from literal margins. Race-level laps require segment count and winner-clock reconciliation. Raw corner groups are carried without inferring per-horse positions. Individual sectional measurements and incident data remain missing. Every roster member is represented, including unstaged or rejected histories.
