# JRA retrospective investigation — 2026-10-23 (READ-ONLY)

## Purpose

Evaluate *why* saved FROZEN predictions missed or captured actual 1st–3rd finishes. Distinguish observable pre-race prior-run facts from hindsight, unsupported claims about trouble, and automatic score changes.

**Scope**: JRA 2026-09-27–2026-10-18. No changes to published predictions, FROZEN, EYE, deployed model, paid APIs, DB, Cron or workers.

## Baseline / cohort

Supabase production SELECT reads joining `public.official_predictions` (status=FROZEN, frozen_at < post_time) to matching `public.official_results` (circuit=JRA, date/track/race_no).
- 110 matched races.
- 329 officially listed first–third finishers (including tie outcomes where applicable).
- 200 of 329 finished in the frozen LAB TOP5; 129 not captured.
- 7 races with *zero* official podium finishers in frozen TOP5.
- 27 races with all three finishers in frozen TOP5.
- Do not misstate the denominator as 330.

## Exploratory candidate: a good result concealed by a 5th–10th finish

From the four past race entries stored in `jra_live_runners.past_runs`, filter:
- past race date < prediction race date, same turf/dirt,
- absolute target–past distance difference <= 400 m,
- prior 5th–10th finish, winner margin 0.0–0.5 sec;
- popularity/odds excluded.

For 110 races, the subset whose current official finish is present comprised 487 LAB-TOP5 runners (200 podium) and 776 outside-TOP5 runners (129 podium). Among outside-TOP5 runners, 149 satisfy the candidate signal; 32 subsequently placed in the current race. A signal does **not** demonstrate a particular rider incident, trip loss, or positive expected betting value.

## Simulated, NON-PRODUCTION swap (rejected)

Experimental operation: where current rank #5 **does not** have this signal and at least one horse rank #6–8 **does**, swap rank #5 with the best-ranked eligible horse. Do **not** alter the stored rankings or LAB EYE. This is a *deliberately naive rule* to test impact.

- 36 proposed swaps among 110 races.
- 4 swaps added an actual podium finisher while losing a non-podium horse.
- 10 swaps lost an actual podium finisher while adding a non-podium horse.
- 1 swap had both runners on podium; 10 neither on podium.
- 11 comparisons have missing recorded finishes for at least one involved runner; treat as **undetermined**, not as losses or wins.
- Among determinate opposite-status swaps, net **-6** podium horses; the naive rule is rejected.

**Counterexample:** 2026-10-17 東京11R 富士S: would elevate 6th-ranked ファーヴェント (current 8th) and displace 5th-ranked エコロアルバ (current 3rd). ファーヴェント had 6th/0.3 sec on the same turf-mile conditions in Apr; エコロアルバ had much better 3rd/0.1 sec in June and an earlier course-distance win. A binary 'close-but-finished-outside-4th' flag is not comparative ability assessment.

**Illustrative successful swap:** 2026-10-17 京都9R 2勝クラス ダ1400: rank7 ホウショウマリス (current 3rd) had a 6th/0.5 sec dirt1400 result on 2026-05-09; rank5 インフローレ (current 14th) had the four listed prior outings on *turf*, with no listed dirt history in those four. This suggests **surface-specific pre-race evidence** matters, but one win does not validate an unconditional swap rule.

## Integrity limitations

1. `jra_live_races.source_checked_at` is after race off for 22 of 110 races (and after frozen_at for 101). Although each historical `past_runs.race_date` in this pilot is filtered to dates before the target race, the stored source snapshot has **not been proven byte-for-byte immutable** from the original forecast. Therefore **exploratory retrospective analysis**, not fully verified blind/as-of backtesting.
2. Many `jra_lab_races.archive_complete` statuses are false despite full result runner lists. Lack of a data field does not prove a horse had no earlier race or race incident.
3. `past_runs.margin_to_winner` for winners can encode winning margin. Qualifying margin signals apply only to *non-winners* (finish >= 5).
4. Horse names alone are not stable identities for linking competitor later records; use horse_ref and race keys.
5. Real second-stage scoring requires a fixed specification, out-of-sample race dates, comparison against all podium picks lost/gained, class/surface subgroup reporting, and no hindsight adjustments.
6. Comments and trouble evidence are optional; no supported automated source was integrated, and missing comments never block baseline evaluation.

## Decision

- **NO-GO** on the simplistic 5th–10th, <=0.5sec promotion.
- **GO** on collecting reproducible *evidence rows* for all horses: course/surface, finishing gap, pace/position, opponent records strictly as-of; no inference that a wide trip or slow break occurred.
- Do not equate retroactively explained wins with prospective predictability or guaranteed improvement.
