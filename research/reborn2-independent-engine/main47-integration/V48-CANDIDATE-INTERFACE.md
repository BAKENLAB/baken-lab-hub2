# REBORN2 V48 research candidate interface

This candidate is research-only. Use the existing verified synthetic intake:
`analyzeVerified(V, request, receipt, {semanticVersion:48, work42Envelope})`.
The prior V43/V45/V46/V47 modes remain unchanged. The output's `candidate`
has schema `REBORN2_V48_RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_V1`. The original runners' current
and formal grades remain null. The candidate's S/A/B/C/UNASSESSED is an ex
ante conditional research judgment, not a formally adopted RANK.

## Inputs and output
- Source-bound synthetic target card, historical result documents and independent
  trusted packet/bundle receipt. Synthetic test fixtures may use self-replayed
  receipts, which do not demonstrate independent official capture.
- All candidate runners have ability, conditional deployment, winning/placing
  paths, counters, held/excluded paths, five concrete axis judgments, 15 EYE
  decisions, coverage, claim-specific missingness and 115 analysis item rows.
- RANK36 exposes conditional indirect comparisons and RANK100 the candidate
  synthesis. Same-field paths retain the existing V47 judgments.
- The cross-field mechanism uses a common peer with matched reported conditions,
  gate/weight, overlapping measured prefix pace and peer response. Retain all
  compatible observations; joint conservative rank bounds are conditional on
  transferring these historical response ranges. No independent pair-vote sum,
  probability, guessed position or exact future dynamics.
- Unknown rivals remain in the field range. A win can be held while a placing
  claim remains supported under the stated assumptions. A single bridge can
  support conditional A/B, not by itself global S or C. S/C require the existing
  stronger same-field review. No S quota. Placing threshold is the existing
  synthetic control, not a canonical production rule.

## Constraints
No official input admission, live-race prospective validation, production
approval, market input, formal grading, deployment, FROZEN, DB or Cron change.
Exact matched-context comparison is deliberately conservative. Changed
conditions, incompatible anchor response, missing data or disconnected
opponents are held; future role and form need not be observed. New-field
interaction is not proved. EYE04/06/14/15 missing requirements remain held.
Category-specific EYE review is not an independent vote; already-consumed
response evidence stays in the bound whether discovery is selected or not.
The original result-boundary checks demonstrate a logical API boundary,
not process/storage isolation. Independent V48 acceptance is separate.
