## RFC3161 custody

The local test timestamps the digest of an evidence envelope and verifies raw modification, trust certificate, nonce, policy OID, certificate validity, timestamping EKU, declared one-second accuracy and cutoff. It proves the local verifier behavior only; it does not prove official-source authenticity, publication time, lawful acquisition, rights or an external timestamp.
