## Meaning of completion
Forecast is not a known future fact. A research-ready pre-race analysis engine must form evidence-backed conditional judgments using information available by cutoff, represent admissible race scenarios and material uncertainty, and give a justified S/A/B/C or unassessed result. It need not know future finish, position or form. Formal adoption and actual predictive-performance validation are separate. A specific historical vector is an antecedent, not a required identical historical race for every horse. Never fabricate splits, causal load, opponent strength, or upcoming result.
